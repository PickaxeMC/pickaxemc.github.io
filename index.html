Contribute in 3 steps

A fix, a translation or an idea: every contribution is welcome, even on your first mod.

1. Fork

Pick a repository, click “Fork” and clone it to your machine.

2. Test

Launch the game from your IDE with Gradle, then change the code.

3. Propose

Open a pull request describing what you changed and why.

Frequently asked questions

      
Answers to the questions we get the most.

      
Which Minecraft versions are supported?
We follow the latest stable release and keep older ones alive when the community needs them. Each repository’s README lists the versions it supports.

      
I’m a beginner: where do I start?
Look for issues labeled “good first issue” in our repositories. They are short and well described, and a maintainer will guide you.

      
What license are the projects released under?
The license is in each repository’s LICENSE file. Most of our projects use MIT or LGPL.

      
How do I report a bug?
Open an issue in the repository concerned, with the game version, the mod loader and your latest.log file.
